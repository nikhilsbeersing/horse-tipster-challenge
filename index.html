Rules & How To Play

Welcome to the private Friends' Horse Racing Tipster League.

1 5,000 Points Per Meeting

Every race meeting gives each tipster a fresh virtual bankroll of exactly 5,000 points.

2 Minimum 100 Pts Stake

You must place a pick with a minimum stake of 100 points on every race in the card. Total stakes cannot exceed 5,000 points.

3 Odds-Based Scoring

If your selected horse wins, you earn Stake × Decimal Odds. If your horse loses, you earn 0 for that stake.

4 Locking Deadlines

Picks for the entire meeting lock 1 minute before Race 1 starts. Make sure your picks are in before the lock time!
